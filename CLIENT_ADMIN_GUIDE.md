## SECTION 2: MANAGING LEADS & ENQUIRIES

Every time someone fills a form on your
website, it appears here automatically.

### How to view new leads:
1. Open your Studio at /studio
2. Click **"🎯 Leads & Enquiries"** 
   in the left menu
3. You will see a table of all enquiries
4. New leads show in **amber/yellow** colour

### How to respond to a lead:
1. Find the lead in the table
2. Click the **green WhatsApp button** 
   next to their name
3. WhatsApp will open with a pre-written
   message — just send it
4. Click **"Mark Contacted"** to update
   their status

### Lead statuses explained:
- 🟡 **New** — Just submitted, not yet contacted
- 🔵 **Contacted** — You have spoken to them
- 🟢 **Enrolled** — They joined a batch
- ⚫ **Not Interested** — Not joining
- 🟠 **Follow Up** — Contact them again later

### How to export leads to Excel:
1. Go to Leads & Enquiries
2. Click **"Export All Leads → CSV"**
3. A file downloads to your computer
4. Open in Excel or Google Sheets

### Email notifications:
Every new lead automatically sends an
email to your registered email address.
The email includes a WhatsApp button
to reply instantly.

## SECTION 3: ADDING A NEW TOPPER/RESULT

When a student passes and becomes a judge:

1. Click **"🏆 Toppers & Results"** 
   in left menu
2. Click **"+ New"** button (top right)
3. Fill in these details:
   - **Full Name** — Student's name
   - **Current Post** — e.g. "Civil Judge"
   - **Course** — Select Civil Judge or APP
   - **College** — Their law college
   - **Batch Year** — e.g. "2024"
   - **District** — Where they are posted
   - **Photo** — Upload their photo
     (click Upload, select image file)
   - **Quote** — A short quote from them
   - **Show on Homepage** — Toggle ON
     to feature them on homepage
4. Click **"Publish"** button (green)
5. Their card appears on your website
   within 1 hour

**Important:** Always get permission
before posting a student's photo and name.

## SECTION 4: ADDING A NEW BATCH

When you open a new batch:

1. Click **"📅 Batches & Schedule"**
   in left menu
2. Click **"+ New"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Course Name** — e.g. 
     "Civil Judge Exam — Full Batch"
   - **Course** — Select from dropdown
   - **Start Date** — Pick from calendar
   - **Timing** — e.g. 
     "7:00 AM – 9:00 AM (Morning)"
   - **Mode** — Online, Offline, or Both
   - **Total Seats** — e.g. 30
   - **Seats Filled** — Start with 0
   - **Status** — Set to "Open"
   - **Show on Website** — Toggle ON
4. Click **"Publish"**

### Updating seats as students join:
From your Dashboard:
- Find the batch in **"Batches & Schedule"**
  widget
- Click **"+"** to add one seat
- Click **"-"** to reduce
- When almost full, click the status badge
  to change to "Filling Fast"
- When full, change to "Full"

## SECTION 5: WRITING A BLOG POST

1. Click **"✍️ Blog Posts"** in left menu
2. Click **"+ New"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Post Title** — e.g. 
     "How to Clear Civil Judge Exam 
     in First Attempt"
   - **URL Slug** — Click "Generate" button
     (auto-fills from title)
   - **Status** — Keep as "Draft" while writing
   - **Published Date** — Set today's date
   - **Category** — Choose from dropdown:
     Exam Guide, Legal Update, Study Material
   - **Tags** — Add keywords like 
     "civil judge, TNPSC, Tamil Nadu"
   - **Cover Image** — Upload a relevant image
   - **Excerpt** — Write 2-3 sentences
     summarising the article
   - **Blog Content** — Write your full article
     in the rich text editor
4. **SEO section** (important!):
   - **SEO Title** — Write a keyword-rich title
     (leave blank to use Post Title)
   - **Meta Description** — 1-2 sentences
     for Google search results
   - **Focus Keyword** — The main keyword
     you want to rank for
5. When ready to publish:
   - Change **Status** to "Published"
   - Click **"Publish"** button

### Rich text editor tips:
- Highlight text and click **B** for bold
- Click the **H** buttons for headings
- Click the image icon to add images
- Use the list icons for bullet points

## SECTION 14: COMMON PROBLEMS & FIXES

### "My change is not showing on the website"
- Wait up to 1 hour for changes to appear
- Make sure you clicked **"Publish"**
  (not just saved a draft)
- If still not showing after 1 hour,
  contact your developer

### "I published but it shows old content"
- The website caches content for speed
- Try clearing your browser cache:
  Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or
  Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
- Wait 1-2 hours and try again

### "A form submission is not in my email"
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Check the Leads table in Studio —
  the lead may be there even if
  email was delayed
- Contact developer if this keeps happening

### "I cannot log in to Studio"
- Go to sanity.io and reset your password
- Or contact your developer

### "I accidentally deleted something"
- Do NOT panic
- Contact your developer immediately
- Sanity keeps a version history of all
  documents — it can be recovered
