SECTION 2: MANAGING LEADS & ENQUIRIES

Every time an aspiring student fills a contact, demo class, or counselling form on the website, it appears here automatically in real time.

How to view new leads:

  1. Open your Studio at /studio.
  2. Click "🎯 Leads & Enquiries" in the left navigation sidebar.
  3. A comprehensive table of all enquiries appears, sorted chronologically.
  4. Fresh, uncontacted leads are prominently highlighted in amber / yellow.

How to respond to a lead:

  1. Locate the student lead row in the table.
  2. Click the green WhatsApp button next to their mobile number.
  3. WhatsApp Web or your WhatsApp app opens with a personalized pre-composed message — simply hit Send.
  4. Click "Mark Contacted" to update their status and log your interaction.

Lead statuses explained:

Keep your admissions pipeline organized using standard color-coded badges:

🟡 New — Just submitted from website, awaiting your first outreach.
🔵 Contacted — You or your admissions counsellor has phoned or messaged them.
🟢 Enrolled — Student has paid fees and successfully joined a batch.
⚫ Not Interested — Candidate decided not to prepare or postponed.
🟠 Follow Up — Student requested to be contacted next week or before next batch.

How to export leads to Excel:

  1. Navigate to Leads & Enquiries.
  2. Click the "Export All Leads → CSV" button in top header.
  3. A clean CSV file downloads to your laptop immediately.
  4. Open directly in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, or Apple Numbers for reporting.
📧 Instant Email Alerts

Every website enquiry immediately triggers an automated notification email to your registered inbox, complete with student course choice, notes, and a direct 1-tap WhatsApp reply button.

SECTION 3: ADDING A NEW TOPPER/RESULT

When an XYZ student cracks the TNPSC Judicial Service or APP examination and secures appointment:

  1. Click "🏆 Toppers & Results" in the left sidebar menu.
  2. Click the "+ New" button in the top right corner.
  3. Fill in the student's credentials:
    • Full Name — Student's full legal name.
    • Current Post — e.g. "Civil Judge" or "Assistant Public Prosecutor (APP)".
    • Course — Select Civil Judge, APP Exam, or other specialized coaching.
    • College — Name of their law college (e.g. Dr. Ambedkar Govt Law College, Chennai).
    • Batch Year — Academic coaching year (e.g. "2024").
    • District — District posting or hometown in Tamil Nadu.
    • Photo — Click Upload and choose a professional high-resolution photo.
    • Quote — A short testimonial quote from the student praising your coaching.
    • Show on Homepage — Toggle ON to feature this topper on the homepage showcase.
  4. Click the green "Publish" button at bottom right.
  5. Their achievement card will automatically display across the website within 1 hour.
⚖️ Legal Compliance Notice

Always obtain explicit verbal or written consent from the candidate before publishing their photograph, rank, and judicial designation on the public web.

SECTION 4: ADDING A NEW BATCH

Whenever you launch a new classroom or online intake for upcoming exams:

  1. Click "📅 Batches & Schedule" in left menu.
  2. Click "+ New".
  3. Enter the batch details:
    • Course Name — e.g. "Civil Judge Exam — Morning Regular Batch".
    • Course — Link to the relevant course dropdown.
    • Start Date — Select commencement date from date picker.
    • Timing — e.g. "7:00 AM – 9:00 AM (Monday to Friday)".
    • Mode — Select "Online", "Offline Classroom", or "Hybrid".
    • Total Seats — Maximum seating capacity (e.g. 30).
    • Seats Filled — Initialize with 0.
    • Status — Set to "Open".
    • Show on Website — Toggle ON.
  4. Click "Publish".

Updating seats as admissions come in:

From your main Studio Dashboard:

  • Find the batch in the "Batches & Schedule" widget.
  • Click "+" to register an enrolled seat.
  • Click "-" to reduce if needed.
  • When remaining seats fall below 5, click the status pill to change to "Filling Fast" (creates high urgency for visitors).
  • When full capacity is reached, change status to "Full".

SECTION 5: WRITING A BLOG POST

Publish regular legal updates, TNPSC notifications, and preparation strategies to grow organic Google traffic.

  1. Click "✍️ Blog Posts" in left menu, then click "+ New".
  2. Fill in post parameters:
    • Post Title — e.g. "How to Clear TNPSC Civil Judge Exam in First Attempt".
    • URL Slug — Click the "Generate" button (automatically creates clean web URL).
    • Status — Keep as "Draft" while writing.
    • Published Date — Set to today's date.
    • Category — Select Exam Guide, Legal Update, or Study Material.
    • Tags — Enter search keywords separated by commas (e.g. "civil judge, TNPSC, Tamil Nadu, BNS").
    • Cover Image — Upload an attractive widescreen banner photo.
    • Excerpt — Write 2 to 3 sentences summarizing the key takeaways.
    • Blog Content — Draft your full article using the rich text editor.
  3. SEO Configuration (Crucial for Google Ranking):
    • SEO Title — Keyword-rich title (leave blank to mirror post title).
    • Meta Description — 1-2 compelling sentences displayed in Google search results.
    • Focus Keyword — Target search term you want to rank #1 for.
  4. When satisfied, change Status to "Published" and click the green "Publish" button.
💡 Rich Text Formatting Tips

Highlight text and press Ctrl+B for bold. Use the H2 / H3 heading buttons to break up sections. Insert legal diagrams with the image button.

SECTION 6: ADDING EXAM NOTIFICATIONS

When TNPSC, the High Court, or Madras Bar Council issues fresh exam notifications or syllabus changes:

  1. Click "🔔 Exam Updates" in the left sidebar menu.
  2. Click "+ New".
  3. Complete the notification fields:
    • Notification Title — Clear concise announcement title.
    • Exam Name — e.g. "TNPSC Civil Judge Notification 2026".
    • Type — Choose: Notification, Result, Admit Card, Syllabus, or News.
    • Details — Brief breakdown of vacancies, age criteria, and key dates.
    • Notification Date — Official gazette release date.
    • Last Date to Apply — Application deadline.
    • Official Link — Direct URL link to official TNPSC PDF or portal.
    • Show as Featured Alert — Toggle ON to trigger urgent top notification bar across website.
  4. Click "Publish".

SECTION 8: UPDATING COURSE FEES & DETAILS

When course structures, fees, or installment schedules are adjusted:

  1. Click "📚 Courses" in the left sidebar menu.
  2. Select the specific course (e.g. "Civil Judge Exam Coaching" or "APP Exam Coaching").
  3. Locate the "Course Fee" field and update the amount (e.g. "₹18,000").
  4. Adjust the "Fee Note" (e.g. "Easy 2-installment EMI available · Inclusive of BNS study material").
  5. Update "Course Duration" (e.g. "6 Months Intensive").
  6. Switch to the "SEO" tab to update meta descriptions if fee structure was advertised.
  7. Click "Publish".
📝 Editing Course Highlights

Under the course edit view, expand the "Course Highlights" list. Click any existing bullet to edit wording, or click "Add Item" to append a new curriculum highlight.

Keep your academy phone, WhatsApp desk, and location accurate at all times.

Method 1 — From Main Studio Dashboard (Fastest):

  1. Open /studio dashboard.
  2. Locate the "Site Settings" widget in the center column.
  3. Modify Phone, WhatsApp, or Email fields directly.

Method 2 — From Left Menu:

  1. Click "⚙️ Site Settings" in the left menu.
  2. Update address, branch coordinates, or phone lines.
  3. Click "Publish" in the top right corner.
⚠️ WhatsApp Format Rule

WhatsApp numbers must always start with country code (91 for India) with NO leading + symbol, spaces, or dashes. Correct Example: 919876543210.

SECTION 12: MANAGING FAQs

  1. Click "❓ General FAQs" in left sidebar menu.
  2. Click "+ New".

SECTION 14: COMMON PROBLEMS & FIXES

1. "My change is not showing on the website"

  • Check Publish Status: Ensure you pressed the green "Publish" button, not just saved as a draft.
  • Cache Invalidation: Content changes synchronize within 1 hour due to high-speed CDN caching.
  • If content has not appeared after 1 hour, contact your technical agency.

2. "I published but my phone/laptop still shows old content"

  • Your personal browser has cached the previous version.
  • Perform a hard refresh: Press Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac).
  • Open an Incognito / Private window to verify the fresh content.

3. "A student form submission did not arrive in my email"

  • Inspect your Email Spam / Junk folders. Mark as "Not Spam" to whitelist future alerts.
  • Check the Leads & Enquiries table inside Sanity Studio — every lead is saved permanently in your database regardless of email delivery.

4. "I cannot log in to Sanity Studio"

  • Navigate to sanity.io and click "Reset Password".
  • Confirm you are using the specific email registered during handover.

5. "I accidentally deleted a post or topper"

  • Do NOT panic. Sanity maintains full version history and point-in-time document snapshots.
  • Contact your developer immediately — deleted documents can be restored from audit logs.
